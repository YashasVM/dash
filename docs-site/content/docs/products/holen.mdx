## What it does

HOLEN lets you share or paste a link, choose a format, and save the download locally. It has an Android app and an interactive terminal flow powered by `yt-dlp`.

**Status:** Active

## Quick start

### Android

Download the [ARM64 APK](https://github.com/YashasVM/HOLEN/releases) and follow the [Android setup guide](https://github.com/YashasVM/HOLEN/tree/main/prod/android).

### Terminal

```bash
npx holen-dl
```

Choose quality, resolution, MP4/MKV, or MP3 from the interactive prompt.

## Privacy

Downloads stay on your device. HOLEN does not bypass DRM, accounts, age gates, access controls, or source-platform terms.
